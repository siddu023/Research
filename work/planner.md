## 2. Capital stack — SBA 504 (verified rules)

**Rules verified (Sept 2026):**
- Structure 50% bank first lien / up to 40% CDC debenture / >=10% equity. Special-purpose property (gas station, car wash both listed) OR new business (<24 months revenue) -> 15% equity, CDC max 35%. Both -> 20% equity, CDC max 30%. Second special-purpose 504 -> 20%. (Alloy Development "Borrower Equity"; ffcfc Equity Injection Q&A; 504capital special-purpose page; SOP 50 10 8 via TMC Financing.)
- Debenture cap $5.0M standard; $5.5M manufacturers/energy public policy; $16.5M aggregate for energy projects. (ffcfc Debenture Limits; WBD.) Effective 2026-07-04, outstanding 7(a) loans no longer reduce 504 availability; combined 7(a)+504 up to $10M (SomerCor "SBA decouples").
- Debenture pricing 2026-09-10: 25-yr 6.54%, 20-yr 6.53%, 10-yr 6.60% effective incl. servicing fees; fixed for term (SomerCor Sept 2026). August was 6.19-6.27%.
- Fees: CDC processing up to 1.5% of net debenture; SBA guaranty 0.5% (FY26); funding ~0.25%; underwriting ~0.4%; bank participation fee 0.5% of first lien (CRS R41184; Pursuit; 504capital; ffcfc FY26 fee notice). ~2.65% financed into the gross debenture + ~0.5-1.25% bank fees.
- Bank first lien: term >=10 yrs when debenture is 20/25-yr; amortization 20-25 yrs typical; rate negotiated (Cornell 13 CFR 120.921; sba504.loans structure page). Prime 6.75% (NerdWallet Sept 2026). Assume 7.50% (7.25-8.00%).
- Eligible project cost includes land, construction, A&E, appraisal/environmental, interim interest, closing costs, F&F, and contingency up to 10% of construction cost (13 CFR 120.882; SEDA-COG; Growth Corp). **Working capital and inventory are NOT eligible** -> equity or 7(a)/line of credit.
