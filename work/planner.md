## 2. Capital stack — SBA 504 (rules verified; rev. Phase 2 cases)

**Rules (Sept 2026):** 50% bank / ≤40% CDC / ≥10% equity; 13 CFR 120.910: 15% if operated ≤2 yrs OR limited/special-purpose building (gas station, car wash), 20% if both; contribution may come from any source except an SBA program — borrowed funds need an outside repayment source; seller financing must be on standby/subordinated (SOP 50 10 8; Starfield & Smith 2025-05; ffcfc). Debenture cap $5.0M ($5.5M energy/manufacturing); 7(a) and 504 decoupled 2026-07-04 (SomerCor). Debenture 25-yr 6.54% / 20-yr 6.53% (2026-09-10). Fees ≈2.65% of net debenture + 0.5% bank participation + 0.5-1% bank origination (CRS R41184; Pursuit; 504capital). Eligible: land, construction, A&E, appraisal/environmental, **interim interest**, closing costs, F&F, **contingency ≤10% of construction** (13 CFR 120.882). **Not eligible:** working capital, inventory, pre-opening payroll → equity or 7(a) companion. New-construction occupancy: ≥60% at closing, ≤20% permanently leased to third parties (13 CFR 120.131) — a Tesla pad (~4% of site) fits with SNDA + CDC/bank consent.

## 4. Delivery model and owner duties (rev. Phase 2 — PR-05/P-01 accepted)

**Why not owner-GC:** G.S. 87-1(b) exempts an owner only if the building is "intended solely for occupancy by that person…firm, or corporation" with a G.S. 87-14 affidavit and a 12-month sole-occupancy presumption (ncleg.net; NCLBGC 2025 Laws & Regs) — EPC/OC ownership plus a Tesla lease breaks "solely." SOP 50 10 8 permits borrower-as-contractor only if experienced and licensed, two bids prove cost ≤ an unaffiliated GC, and no profit; SBA calls DIY "generally unsatisfactory" (Partner ESI; ffcfc; Starfield & Smith 2025-10). Two first-time owners fail the experience test regardless.

**Structure carried:** NC-licensed GC as **CM-agency** (licensed contractor of record; G.S. 87-1(a) covers one who "undertakes to superintend or manage" construction ≥$40K — counsel to confirm bank acceptance vs CM-at-risk); owner-held fixed-price subcontracts bid open-book (two bids satisfy SOP); **owner-furnished equipment packages** (procurement $2.16M: tunnel, fuel, canopy, vacs, DCFC) with manufacturer install — the saving ($115-230K avoided GC markup, auditor §5) is captured here and never shown as a credit line. CM-at-risk/GMP alternative: fee 2.5-3.95% + GMP contingency 3-5% (Law Insider samples; Angi 2026) ≈ $100-200K more; use only if two CDCs reject CM-agency in writing. OPEN RISK until a term sheet says so.
