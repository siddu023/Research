# RS Fuels Flagship — Architect's Phase 1 Deliverable

Author: architect agent. Date: 2026-09-14. Status: Phase 2 revised (auditor challenges answered in work/TASKS.md; changed lines are marked "rev. Phase 2"; revision log in Sec. 7).

Conventions: every dollar figure carries LOW / EXPECTED / HIGH and a source with date. Where no public figure exists I say so and give the estimating basis ("EB:"). "VFP" = vehicle fueling position (one hose side); "MPD" = multi-product dispenser (2 VFP). "bd" = business days.

Summary of the recommended program (details and options below):

| Item | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lot | 2.75 ac target (2.5 ac is feasible only with underground detention) | 2.5 ac + surface pond does not fit a 15-car stack plus tanker loop |
| Fuel | 8 VFP (4 MPD, 2 islands) with canopy/piping designed for 12 VFP | Marginal cost of positions is low vs. store; 6 VFP starves the "traffic magnet" |
| Store | 4,000 sf, food program in ~600 sf kitchen | 3,600 sf saves ~$120K but constrains kitchen + cooler; 4,500 sf adds $150K+ with no revenue proof at this trade area |
| Tunnel | 120 ft conveyor in a 150 x 40 ft PEMB shell, mid-tier equipment with blank arch positions | Length cannot be added later; arches can |
| Stack | 2 pay lanes x 4 cars + single 12-car merge lane = 16-20 cars off-street | #1 tunnel failure mode is queue spill |
| Vacuums | 10 stalls, 2 rows of 5 under canopy, central vac | Under 8 stalls, members queue for vacs on Saturdays |
| EV | Tesla host pad reserved for 8 stalls; 2 dual DCFC (4 ports); 6 L2 in regular parking | Matches Tesla's "starting around eight" host norm |
| Stormwater | Surface SCM on 3 ac; underground on 2.5 ac | Land price decides this line |

The 2018 NC State Building Code family (based on the 2015 I-codes) remains in force; the 2024 NC codes (based on 2021 I-codes) were delayed by S.L. 2024-57 and S.L. 2025-2 and take effect 12 months after the State Fire Marshal certifies readiness; earliest possible date Jan 1, 2027, not confirmed. Sources: NC OSFM, Apr 7 2025 https://www.ncosfm.gov/news/press-releases/2025/04/07/north-carolina-delays-implementation-2024-state-building-code ; NC Licensing Board update Nov 11 2025 https://nclicensing.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/UPDATE-ON-NEW-NORTH-CAROLINA-STATE-BUILDING-CODE-EFFECTIVE-DATES-2025-11-11.pdf . Design to 2018 NC; a permit pulled in 2027 may land under 2024 NC. Planner: this is a schedule risk worth a line.

Conditions under which 18 months is achievable (each must be met, not assumed): (1) by-right parcel with an existing turn lane and no stream buffer; (2) Duke Energy application at concept stage (month 1) with the split-service design in Sec. 2.8 so the store opens on the initial 1,200 A service and tunnel/DCFC energize on later transformers; (3) Mecklenburg Express/OnSchedule review or Raleigh SPR overlapped with the building set (saves 4-6 weeks); (4) UST-6 filed with the building set, and tank/canopy/tunnel POs released by month 6-7 (PR-note-9); (5) PEMB shells for both buildings; (6) CM with fixed-price subs so NCDEQ hold-point inspections do not idle other trades. Miss any two and the schedule is 20+ months.

## 6. Assumptions and open items for other agents

Assumptions:
- A1: Parcel is zoned for vehicle fuel sales and car wash by right (Charlotte UDO and Raleigh UDO district tables were not retrievable; verify per parcel). Conditional rezoning adds 6-9 months.
- A2 (rev. Phase 2, PR-04, OPEN RISK with decision rule): Tesla "Host a Supercharger" (Tesla-funded) is assumed still offered alongside "Supercharger for Business" (host-funded; $940K all-in for 8 V4 posts = $500K hardware + $440K install, plus $0.10/kWh, Electrek Apr 8 2026; Basenor Mar 2026 https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/tesla-supercharger-for-business-full-program-breakdown). Neither April-2026 article confirms the funded track; the primary Tesla page returned 403. Decision rule: apply at LOI; if no Tesla site offer by building-permit issuance, install conduit sleeves only ($15K / $20K / $25K), no self-funded Superchargers, owned DCFC capped at 2 dual units on a separate Duke make-ready transformer. The 8 reserved stalls serve as store overflow parking meanwhile. Tesla easement/lease requires CDC/bank consent (planner X-08).
- A3: Fuel storage: 4 tanks per brief. Dimensions assume 20K/20K/12K/12K; procurement to confirm tank OD and length, which set the pit and the tanker pad.
- A4: No streams, wetlands or floodplain on the parcel (buffers of 30-200 ft would break the fit on 2.5-2.75 ac).
- A5: 2018 NC codes govern; if permit issuance slips into 2027 the 2024 NC codes may apply.

Needed from procurement:
- P1: Tunnel vendor building envelope (tunnel width 14 vs 16 ft, equipment room area, trench depth, roof height) for the 120 ft package; my 150 x 40 ft shell is from Tommy's 135 x 40 ft published model.
- P2: Vacuum producer size and whether 10 drops fit one producer; canopy geometry.
- P3: Pay-station count (2 vs 3 lanes) and gate type; affects stacking geometry.
- P4: DCFC power cabinet footprint and kW; sets transformer pad and Duke service size.
- P5: Written Duke Energy transformer lead time.

Needed from planner:
- S1: Whether the 18-month horizon is fixed. My permitting critical path is 20-24 months without rezoning.
- S2: Whether Tesla energization is allowed to trail opening (my recommendation: yes).

Anticipated auditor challenges and my position:
- "Store $300/sf is high; Terrapin says $150-350." Position: the $150 end is a 3,000 sf beer-and-cigarettes box; a food-program store with kitchen exhaust, grease interceptor and 16-20 cooler doors is not that. I will move to $275 if procurement produces an NC bid comp.
- "Tunnel shell at $150/sf is high for PEMB." Position: PEMB kit-and-erect is $28-50/sf (Terrapin PEMB guide 2026 https://terrapincg.com/news/pre-engineered-metal-building-cost-per-square-foot-usa), but a wash bay adds the conveyor trench, pit, glazing walls, epoxy, floor heat and the equipment room MEP rough-in; $110-150/sf is the vendor-published tunnel range.
- "Paving $637K is a big line." Position: it is; 67,000 sf of hardscape is the price of a 15-car stack plus a tanker loop. Cut the stack and the wash dies.
